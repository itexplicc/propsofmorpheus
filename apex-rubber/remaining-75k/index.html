Keep Ron, but use Ron pricing as a controlled market-entry lane.

Ron is useful

Ron helps prove market movement against China. Keeping him makes sense because he was early and he validates the price point.

Pro must be limited

At $0.66, Pro should not be pushed as a standalone high-volume product until max capacity is reached or until the mix supports it.

Standard carries entry

Standard/Game at $0.59 becomes a real contribution product after the $75K unlocks higher capacity and lower landed cost.

Smart position to George: we are not trying to sell above China to enter the market. We are using the $75K to make APEX cost low enough to compete at China-entry pricing.

4. Rules to win against China

Do this without sounding naive.

Rule 1: Keep Pro around 20% of entry mix

At current Ron price, Pro at 20% is manageable after the first stage and profitable at max stage. Pro-heavy orders need separate pricing or a blended adjustment.

Rule 2: Do not give free extras inside base puck price

Printing, packaging changes, special handling and special terms must be charged separately or protected inside the blended price.

Rule 3: Push Standard/Game volume first

Standard/Game creates the main margin at China-entry pricing. This is the product that opens doors against China.

Rule 4: Use the $75K to lower cost, not raise price

The $75K matters because it turns Ron/China-entry pricing from dangerous pricing into workable pricing.
