4. Recommended Ron rule

Keep Ron, but limit him.

What we can continue

Keep Ron as a legacy relationship account because he was there from the beginning. Allow limited Standard/Game volume where the cost stage gives contribution.

What we should not do

Do not let Ron pricing become the benchmark for APEX. Do not sell standalone Pro at $0.66. Do not increase Pro volume unless Pro is repriced.

Suggested limit: cap Ron around 7,500–10,000 pucks per order/month, keep Pro near 20% of the mix, and require a higher Pro price if he wants more Pro volume.
