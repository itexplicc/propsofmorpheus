3. Subtle Ron recommendation

Keep Ron, but do not let Ron set the company price.

How to keep him

Keep Ron as an early-supporter relationship account with a reserved allocation. This respects the relationship without turning the whole factory into a low-margin legacy supplier.

How to protect APEX

Review his mix and pricing as capacity improves. Keep Pro controlled at the legacy price because Pro carries tighter margin and uses more expensive raw material.

Suggested wording: "Ron remains a supported legacy account, but future growth capacity should be directed toward better-paying team, wholesale, retail and distributor channels."
