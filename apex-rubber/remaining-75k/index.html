3. Why Pro looks heavy

Pro is the bottleneck product, not a failed product.

Why Pro is high now

Pro is still being produced in lower quantity through the old 9-cavity Pro mold. It also uses more expensive raw materials and tighter production control. Low Pro quantity spreads overhead across fewer Pro pucks.

Why the past combined orders hurt

Earlier Pro and Game/Standard orders were treated as one production cycle while the factory was still absorbing unexpected raw-material purchases, interruptions, corrections, trials, freight pressure and setup delays. Those costs made the combined burden heavier than planned.

This is exactly why the Pro mold and machine-readiness stage matters. Pro moves from $0.91 landed to $0.76, then toward $0.618.
