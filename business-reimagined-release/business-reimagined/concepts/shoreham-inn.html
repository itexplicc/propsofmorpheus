Why this direction

The inn's public site offers main-house rooms, separate sheep-barn loft suites and an on-site pub. The proposed direction makes those choices easy to explore.

Suggested website scope

$1,000 for an agreed 3-5 page responsive website. 50% upfront and 50% at completion; target 2-5 working days after approved materials and an available slot. Existing booking remains linked; third-party fees and system redevelopment excluded.
