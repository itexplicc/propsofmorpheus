Why this direction

CPM's public site offers drawing-based RFQs for casting programs and in-house tooling. This proposed structure connects that existing offer to a clearer capabilities journey.

Suggested website scope

$3,000 for an agreed multi-page refresh. 50% upfront; around 10 working days after client materials, scope and a production slot are ready. Advanced integrations, large catalogs and platform migrations quoted separately.
