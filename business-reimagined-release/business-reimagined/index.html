What’s included, what’s separate, and what we need from you +

A scope you can rely on

Each revision round is one consolidated set of changes within the agreed direction. New pages, changed requirements and additional functionality are scoped separately. Delivery estimates start when your materials and approvals are ready and a production slot is confirmed.

Third-party costs & larger builds

Domains, hosting, platform fees, subscriptions, licensed assets and paid services are separate. Ecommerce is not bundled into the $350 or $1,000 packages. Advanced cart or checkout changes, complex Shopify migrations, large catalogs, subscriptions, custom apps and extensive integrations are quoted separately, including for the $3,000 package.

Your part of the process

You supply your logo and brand assets, approved copy, images you have permission to use, business details and required account access. We confirm requirements, scope, payment terms and available capacity before a project starts. Copywriting, photography and ongoing maintenance can be discussed separately.
