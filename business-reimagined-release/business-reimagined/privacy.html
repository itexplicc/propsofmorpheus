Morpheus PD / Privacy notice

Your idea. Your details.

When you submit a website inquiry, we collect your name, email address, business name, optional website, selected package, project brief and privacy consent. We use these details to respond to your inquiry and manage the project discussion.

Your inquiry is sent to our team through a transactional email provider and may be recorded in our private inquiry tracker. We do not treat an inquiry as consent to unrelated marketing. Access is limited to people and service providers needed to handle the inquiry.

We use basic security checks and a hidden spam field. Your browser temporarily stores an inquiry request identifier in session storage to protect retries against duplicate submissions. Hosting and email providers may process technical information such as request timestamps and IP addresses for security and delivery.

Portfolio images are screenshots of public project pages. A screenshot service may be used by our server to refresh those public-page captures. Your inquiry details are never sent to that screenshot service. External project links open websites with their own policies.

We retain inquiry records only as needed for the project discussion, legitimate business records and applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion, contact info@morpheuspd.io.
