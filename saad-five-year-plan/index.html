Morpheus ownership ladder

Factory ownership becomes a staircase.

Entrepreneurs enter at the level they can afford, then earn the right to climb. Assets, cost rights, governance, liabilities and exits are explicit.

Tier 1 · Lowest entry

Mould / tooling owner

  • Entrepreneur owns product-specific tooling.
  • Production uses an approved existing location.
  • Clear rate, quality, priority and IP agreement.
  • Best for demand proof before heavy capex.
Tier 2

Dedicated production cell

  • Entrepreneur owns dedicated machines or line assets.
  • Shared premises, staff and services reduce overhead.
  • Morpheus tracks output, cost, quality and downtime.
  • Expandable after stable demand.
Tier 3

Managed factory unit

  • Dedicated staff, machines, stock and capacity.
  • Group manages finance, procurement and logistics.
  • Owner sees live decisions and operating data.
  • Separate unit P&L and cash discipline.
Tier 4 · Highest

25 / 75 joint company

  • Named company owns land, machines, staff, vehicles and account.
  • Entrepreneur begins with 25%; group owns and operates 75%.
  • Full visibility and controlled approvals.
  • Lifetime product-at-cost rights are legally defined.
“Lifetime at cost” must be precise: direct, factory, fully absorbed, or cost plus management fee; also minimum volume, quality, payment, capacity priority, IP, transfers, death, sale, dispute and shutdown.

Non-negotiable venture gates

No factory begins with excitement alone.

A venture advances only when every gate is green.

1

Demand

Paid test, PO, repeat sales or credible 12-month forecast.

2

Unit economics

Positive contribution under conservative volume, rejects, freight and collection.

3

Capital

Staged release, 20% contingency and evidence against every use.

4

Operator

A named person owns the P&L before assets are committed.

5

Governance

Shares, board, bank, salaries, dividends, dilution, exit and disputes signed.

6

Morpheus data

Orders, payments, assets, approvals, quality, inventory and shipments from day one.

Automatic stop conditions
Stop or redesign after three missed monthly milestones, negative conservative economics, no accountable operator, incomplete statutory records, dangerous customer concentration, or capital requests without evidence.
